The draft is `draft.txt` in this folder. Read it. Beside it sit one or more glossaries,
`glossary-*.md`: each holds the terms this team uses, a definition under each, and under
`_Avoid_` the words that are not to stand in for it. Read them.

Review the draft's nouns against the glossaries:

- every word in the draft that a glossary lists under `_Avoid_`, with the term that replaces it
- every term the draft uses for something a glossary defines under another name
- every name the draft coins for a thing the reader's industry or app already has a word for;
  give that word, and say where it comes from
- every thing the draft names that no glossary defines and no industry term covers; say that it
  has no name, so the writer can describe it in plain words

Give each with the line it sits on. Where the draft and the glossaries agree, say nothing.
Answer under these headings, nothing else: Avoided words · Other names · Coined · Unnamed.
